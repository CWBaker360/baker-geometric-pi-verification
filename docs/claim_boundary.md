# Claim Boundary

The strongest supported computational statement is:

> At 120-decimal-digit working precision, the deterministically constructed
> value \(\pi_g\) is consistent with the listed bounded series, product,
> asymptotic, analytic, and special-function relations under the stated
> acceptance rules.

The suite does not establish functional identity across all mathematical
domains and does not prove \(\pi_g=\pi\). Special-function checks may depend
internally on classical \(\pi\) through the numerical library.
